# Gym Tracker 

A comprehensive fitness tracking application built with [Expo](https://expo.dev) that helps you manage your workout routines, track your progress, and stay organized with scheduling and timing features.

## Features

### Workout Plans
Create and manage personalized workout plans tailored to your fitness goals. Organize exercises by sets, reps, and difficulty levels. Easily add, edit, and delete exercises from your plans to keep your routines fresh and challenging.

### Calendar
View your workout schedule on an interactive calendar. Track which days you've completed workouts and plan ahead for future sessions. Stay motivated by seeing your workout history and consistency at a glance.

### Interval Timer
Built-in interval timer for timed workouts and rest periods. Perfect for HIIT training, circuit workouts, or any time-based exercises. Keep your workouts structured and efficient.
